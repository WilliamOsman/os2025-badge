1) Double click 'RUN_THIS.sh'

2) Insert a new chip into the teal holder on the desk:
	- Align the dot and notch with drawing on orange pad
	- Lift ZIF handle up to open, handle down to lock chip in

3) Press the <ENTER> key on the keyboard

4) Wait for chip to program and verify

5) Remove programmed chip

6) Repeat from step 2
